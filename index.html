About Us

True American Electric was founded by three electricians from different parts of the country who ultimately found their way to East Tennessee. Between us, we bring more than 45 years of experience in the electrical industry and over 55 years of combined experience across the trades. Over the years, we've had the opportunity to work on a wide variety of projects, learn from talented people, and gain an appreciation for the value of doing things right the first time. Today, our primary focus is custom homes, new construction, a dash of commercial and electrical service installations throughout the Greater Knoxville area. We enjoy working with homeowners, builders, and developers to create electrical systems that are practical, dependable, and built around the needs of each project. As the industry continues to move faster, we've seen quality become less of a priority on many projects. We believe experience, planning, and communication make it possible to deliver both without sacrificing one for the other. At the end of the day, we're just a group of experienced tradesmen who enjoy what we do. We believe in treating people fairly, being easy to work with, and letting our work speak for itself. We're proud of what we've built, grateful for the people who trust us with their projects, and look forward to serving the Greater Knoxville area for years to come.
